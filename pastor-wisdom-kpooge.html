Biography

Amb. Major Prophet Wisdom Kpooge was born on 23 September 1983 in Barako community, Gokana Local Government Area, Rivers State, Nigeria.

He serves as the Pastor and leader of Omega Praise and Miracle Center (OPMC), a Christian ministry established in 2021 and located in Eteo community, Eleme Local Government Area, Rivers State, Nigeria.

He is also known as Mene Bari 1 of Africa.
